# Checkpoint C1 — flash + monitor on the C3 and C6 (2026-10-20/21)

**Covers:** roadmap checkpoint C1 ([11](../spec/11-roadmap.md)) and the USB-Serial/JTAG reset rows of spike S1 ([04 §4.4](../spec/04-boards.md)).

**Flow under test** (CLI from this repo, hub on `127.0.0.1:7420`):

```
fr doctor
fr build --profile <c3o-pad|c6-pad>
fr flash --port <usb-serial> --profile <profile> --plan full     # then --plan app
fr wait log <device> "fr: ready" --since <cursor from flash>
fr logs <device>
fr devices reset <device> [--mode bootloader]
```

## Devices

| Board | Device id | Chip (boot log) | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESP32-C3 0.42" OLED | `1cdbd416ba34` | ESP32-C3 v0.4, efuse block v1.3, 4 MB | `c3o-pad` |
| ESP32-C6 Super Mini | `58e6c5d55e64` | ESP32-C6 v0.2, efuse block v0.3, 4 MB | `c6-pad` |

## Results

| Step | C3 | C6 |
|---|---|---|
| `fr build` (clean / incremental) | 30 s / 9 s | 8 s incremental, 198 KB app (10 % of the slot) |
| `fr flash --plan full` | 2.0 s | 1.7 s |
| `fr flash --plan app` | 1.9 s | ✓ |
| Boot banner after flash | `fr: ready id=1cdbd416ba34 fw=… state=UNASSIGNED … reset=USB slot=ota_0` | `fr: ready id=58e6c5d55e64 fw=d60e7f740485 state=UNASSIGNED … board=esp32c6-supermini` |
| `fw` in the banner = build `fw_id[0:12]` | ✓ | ✓ |
| Hub reset (`fr devices reset`) | `rst:0x15 (USB_UART_CHIP_RESET)`, port stays open | `rst:0x15 (USB_UART_HPSYS)`, port stays open |
| Bootloader round trip | — | `--mode bootloader`, then a hard reset: banner again |
| Wrong-chip image | C6 image on the C3: `TARGET_MISMATCH`, nothing written, board keeps running | — |
| Stop guard | `fr hub stop` during a build: `LOCKED`, exit 5 | — |

The whole boot log, from `ESP-ROM:` to the banner, is captured because the hub resets the chip through its own open port after esptool finishes with `--after no-reset` ([06 §6.4](../spec/06-identity-ota.md)).

## Findings

1. **Boot log lost with esptool's reset (C3).** esptool's `--after hard-reset` rebooted the chip while nobody had the port open, and the USJ console dropped the banner. Fixed: the hub resets after it reopens the port.
2. **A blank C6 floods the log.** Factory-blank (and `erase-flash`ed) C6 flash makes the ROM print `invalid header: 0xffffffff` ~400 times a second, with a `rst:0x7 (TG0_WDT_HPSYS)` watchdog reset every ~0.7 s (~270 lines per cycle). Fixed: the hub collapses consecutive identical lines (`(previous line repeated N more times)`, once a second), and splits the `ESP-ROM:` banner off the line the reset cut short ([08 §8.3](../spec/08-hub.md)).
3. **`flash` answered `NOT_FOUND` once on the fresh C6.** In the first minute after the board was plugged in, its port closed and reopened every 2–3 s, and one `fr flash` landed in a gap. The flapping did not come back on the same boot loop: a bare serialport reader saw 0 closes in 8 s, both with and without a SQLite insert per line, so neither the hub's write load nor the ROM loop explains it. The likely cause is the plug-in itself (cable or enumeration). Mitigated: ops that act on a port wait up to 5 s for a port that vanished in the last 60 s.

## Not covered

- Both boards attached at once: the C3 was unplugged to make room for the C6.
