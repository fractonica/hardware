# Checkpoint C5 — ESP-NOW, bindings and flooding on the C3 and C6 (2026-10-22)

**Covers:** roadmap checkpoint C5 ([11](../spec/11-roadmap.md)) and gates **T-NN**, **T-FLOOD** and **T-FLOOD-B** ([05 §5.18](../spec/05-network.md)). Design and contracts: [docs/design/c5-espnow.md](../design/c5-espnow.md).

**Setup:** hub on the MacBook (Wi-Fi, router on channel 1). Boards: `pad-c3` (C3 #2 `1cdbd4175cc4`, addr 1, on a USB charger, Wi-Fi only), `pad-c3b` (C3 #1 `1cdbd416ba34`, addr 3, on the Mac), `pad-c6` (C6 `58e6c5d55e64`, addr 2, on the Mac). All three within about a metre of each other; ESP-NOW neighbour RSSI −40…−74 dBm. Firmware went on by `fr deploy all` (Wi-Fi for `pad-c3`, serial for the others).

## T-FLOOD-B — passed with the `wifi_down` hook

The router stayed on (owner's choice): `net wifi_down 120` on all three boards (STA disconnected, no join or scan, radio held on channel 1), while the hub sent `message_send broadcast` at 1 Hz. `pad-c3b` and `pad-c6` kept their USB serial sessions; `pad-c3` had only ESP-NOW.

| Run | Delivered (`pad-c3` / `pad-c3b` / `pad-c6`) | Back on TCP after the hook |
|---|---|---|
| First firmware, 115 broadcasts | 112 / 115 / 114 | < 10 s |
| After the fix round, 120 broadcasts | **120 / 120 / 120** | **≈ 3 s** (all three) |

## Findings

1. **K counting the hub copy left the cut-off node with one air copy.** With "the TCP copy counts as one", a hub-linked node cancels its seed after hearing one air copy, so about 76 % of broadcasts went on the air once and a node with no hub link lost whatever one frame lost (~1.5 % at −70 dBm on these C3 boards). Now only copies heard over ESP-NOW count toward K = 2; two seeders in range both transmit, and the cut-off node misses about PER² ([05 §5.9](../spec/05-network.md)).
2. **A USB node whose Wi-Fi died got hub broadcasts on the dead TCP for 2 intervals,** and the later repairs (`ttl_remaining` 1) are never seeded, so the Wi-Fi-only node missed them. Hub broadcasts and repairs now go on every session of a node, standby serial included; the node delivers once and the extra copies neither seed nor count ([05 §5.9–5.10](../spec/05-network.md)).
3. **The all-channel sweep could livelock in a full outage** (neighbours kept each other from sweeping) — found in review. The sweep is now bounded: never while a neighbour reports Wi-Fi up, else after 5 min (60 s when nothing is heard) ([05 §5.4](../spec/05-network.md)).
4. The adversarial review confirmed 12 issues in all, fixed before the final runs: RESOLVE answers that could wipe the directory, a same-run WELCOME moving the BCAST window back, a new source's floor ACKing reordered frames, fast sends with no path reporting success, late ESP-NOW send callbacks credited to the wrong frame, the trial pin overwriting the base pin, the binding table outgrowing `bind_list`, bindings on `fr.node` events, and MSG data re-parsed on re-resolution.
5. `fr diag <alias>` on a C3 at −77 dBm can time out over TCP while the same command by `/dev` path answers at once over serial. Not new; the RF note from C4 stands.
