## Findings

1. **S3: a frame on the end of a log line** (bench fix 1). On the dual-core S3, the Wi-Fi blob printed one log line in two `vprintf` calls, and `fr_serial`'s writer slipped a frame between them: `I (437) wifi:wifi certification version: v7.0@fr1:AaEA…`. The hub took the whole line as a log line, so the `node.reboot` RESULT was lost and `command_send` ended `DEVICE_OFFLINE`. Fixed on both ends ([05 §5.12.3](../spec/05-network.md)):
   - `firmware/components/fr_serial/src/serial.cpp`: the writer closes an open log line (`\n`) before it writes a frame;
   - `packages/hub/src/serial.ts`: a line with `@fr1:` after its start is split into the log text and the frame (test in `packages/hub/test/runtime.test.ts`).

   After the fix pad-s3 passed 5/5 reboots.
2. **T-FLOOD-B isolated a node it could not reach** (bench fix 2). `planOutage` in `tests/e2e/t-flood-b.ts` dropped the default node's hub link (`drop_hub_link`) even when it was not on USB; a Wi-Fi-only node is ESP-NOW only under its `wifi_down` anyway, and isolating it first left the hub no way to arm that hook. Now only a node on a USB port the hub holds is isolated; otherwise the run notes it and isolates none. Test in `e2e-gates-c.test.ts`.
3. **T-NN read a gauge as a counter** (bench fix 3). `wentBack` in `tests/e2e/lib/nn-flood-stats.ts` flagged any counter that dropped as a reboot, including `stats bindings` `rtt_p50_us`, a median (4,473 → 4,406 µs). Gauges are now skipped. Stopped run 2 (6 fired for 5 asked, all via espnow, 0 failed) passes under the fixed rule (the test in `e2e-gates-c.test.ts` uses its figures); run 1 (3 fired for 5 asked, presses outside the window) is recorded as a miss.
4. **T-INPUT reorders an event without injected loss.** 2 of the 4 sim runs after fix 1 had one reordered event on one or two boards, out of 170: flagged `late`, never lost. A single lost UDP frame's retry arrives after the next event. Frames are delivered at once ([05 §5.8](../spec/05-network.md), *Ordering*); the 50 ms reorder buffer for `ordered` ports is not built, and `fr.button` is not ordered. Open for M2. One more run had `busy` on the first press of three boards (not traced).
5. **The C3 radio is still the weak link.** The C3s sat at −70…−80 dBm to the AP (pad-c3 −71…−80, once −66 in T-NN run 2; pad-c3b −70…−76), with the RSSI warning in nearly every gate, and pad-s3 hears pad-c3 at −83…−85 dBm over ESP-NOW. Every gate passed on them anyway; pad-c3's Wi-Fi OTA took 16.6 s at −80 dBm (at C4 pad-c3b took 52 s at −76).
6. **Two data ports for four boards.** Only two Mac USB ports carried data; the USB hub's port gave power only after a while, and the C6 dropped off it. Effects:
   - the USB phases ran on two boards at a time, and pad-c3 never had a data port;
   - pad-c3b went back in without a data link after T-PRES unplug: no `port.attached`, so no replug time, and T-PORTS run 1 failed it until it was moved back;
   - hooks armed on a Wi-Fi-only node could not be ended early: T-FLOOD's pad-c3 came back only when its 200 s hook ran out, and T-FLOOD-B's pad-c3 and pad-c6 51 s after the run.
7. **Hub stalls:** none recorded. The re-run's reports carry no loop-delay figure, and T-LAT, the gate the stalls hurt at C6, was not re-run.
8. **Dashboard wizard:** the stage markers stayed `pending` while flashing and all turned done at the end (demo step 2), so the wizard shows no progress during the flash.
