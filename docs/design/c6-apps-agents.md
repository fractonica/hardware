## 1. Scope

**In C6:**
- **SDK:** `@fractonica/sdk`, the `/app` endpoint (`fr-app/1`), `client_generate` with `fr codegen client`, the generated typed facades, and `web/demo`.
- **Dashboard:** `packages/dashboard`, the hub's `/ws`, static serving of `/` and `/apps/<name>/`, the web guard, the new bus events, the `hub_status` additions, the LNP self-test, the `_fractonica._tcp` advert, the event-loop warning, the registry ops, `hub_doctor`, and OpenAPI.
- **Agents:** `fr mcp`, `plugin/` with 3 skills, a SessionStart hook and AGENTS.md, plus `logs_assert`, `test_run` with `tests/e2e`, and the parity test.
- **Gate support:**
  - T-PORTS: serial leases, external-holder detection, and a Web Serial fixture.
  - T-INPUT: `prompt_human`, `net loss`, the router's late/resync counters and resync rule, and the harnesses.
  - T-LAT: `pin_pulse`, the `test_pin` codegen, node `exec_at`, the receive-time stamp, `latency_probe`, and the harness.
- **Node discovery:** the `_fractonica._tcp` query ([05 §5.12.5](../spec/05-network.md) step 4).
- **Traits:** written into `fr_manifest` caps.

**Not in C6** (they stay open in 11):
- LOG frames from Wi-Fi nodes.
- Crash reports, with `crash_list` and `crash_get`. The dashboard shows "not built".
- Resource-style REST routes ([09 §9.1](../spec/09-agent-api.md) and 02 Flow B are edited to say they are deferred).
- `--listen-app` and `--listen-api`.
- Node → node `exec_at`: it stays `UNSUPPORTED`, deferred to M2 with BEACON hub time.
- Binary SDK mode.
- `device_forget`, `flash_erase` and `test_report`.

### 7.11 Bench round (2026-10-22/23)

All four gates passed on the C3s and the C6, T-LAT's pad-c6 → pad-c3b direction on a re-run, and M1 demo steps 3–5 passed; the figures are in the [spike note](../spikes/2026-10-23-c6-apps-agents.md). Two fixes came out of it, each with a host test:

| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **A UDP-only Q1 event was lost with TCP up.** pad-c3b at −77 dBm: 3 events per T-INPUT sim run ended `delivery_failed` after their UDP attempts, although a TCP session was up. | Firmware (`fr_net` `Net::service_pending`): a Q1 frame that went over UDP alone and ran out of UDP attempts, if it is not stale (`event_max_age_ms`) and a TCP session is up, gets one copy over TCP and then TCP's `tcp_delivery_ms` deadline, instead of `delivery_failed`; stale input is still dropped (`events_dropped`). | B2 | **Fresh input flagged `late` after a quiet spell** (demo step 3). Observations (open in [11 §11.4](../spec/11-roadmap.md)):
- **C3 placement.** The C3 OLED boards' uplink (8.5 dBm TX cap) dominates the gate figures. At −74…−78 dBm (TCP flapping, about 8 % UDP retransmission) T-INPUT showed late and lost events, and T-LAT's first pad-c6 → pad-c3b run failed (p50 11.70 ms, 88.2 % matched, pad-c3b at −74 dBm); moved toward the router (−61…−67 dBm), both passed.
