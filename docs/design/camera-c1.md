# CAM-1: a camera module (`fr.camera`) on the AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM, the classic ESP32 as a target, and `web/cam`

> **Proposed (2026-11-03): nothing built yet but the spike (§9).** The fourth project step on Fractonica, after [A1](audio-a1.md), [A2](audio-a2-listener.md) and [CSI-1](csi-c1.md). Run "lighter": this note, stream C, then F, H and W in parallel, one review pass, a fix round, the bench. Your decisions wait in §8; numbers marked **proposed** wait for your approval. Anything marked *inferred* was not read in code, a datasheet or on the bench.

**What you asked for.** A camera module that is robust, reusable and stable, like the audio and CSI ones: the board photographs on request (to the Mac), streams a live view, and records to its micro-SD card on its own (a timelapse or a burst) for the Mac to collect later. Everything through the same capability model, hub ops, CLI and SDK as the rest, so an agent drives it with `fr camera …` and a web app shows it.

**The board** (your photo and esptool, 2026-11-03): an **AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM** (silkscreen "ESP32-CAM"): ESP32-D0WD-V3 rev 3.1, 40 MHz crystal, 4 MB flash (DIO at 40 MHz, 3.3 V by the strapping pin), **4 MB quad PSRAM** (found at boot), an **OV2640** on the FPC (PID 0x26, VER 0x42), a micro-SD slot on the SD_MMC lines, a white flash LED (GPIO4, active high, bright), a red LED on the back (GPIO33, active low), a RESET button, **no BOOT button and no USB**: it sits on the **ESP32-CAM-MB** base, whose CH340 (USB 1a86:7523, `/dev/cu.usbserial-110`) carries UART0 and the EN/IO0 auto-reset. Headers (photo): left `5V GND IO12 IO13 IO15 IO14 IO2 IO4`, right `3V3 IO16 IO0 GND VCC U0R U0T GND/R`. The card in it: a 32 GB SDHC "SD16G".

**What exists / what is missing** (survey, 2026-11-03):
- **No classic ESP32 anywhere**: `ChipId` (`schema/protocol.yaml`), the hub's chip tables, codegen's per-chip tables, every driver's `targets:`, `firmware/idf-version.lock`'s targets, and `fr_diag`'s download-mode restart know C3/C5/C6/S3 only. Spec 11 says the WROOM boards are not used.
- **Serial is USB-Serial/JTAG only**: `fr_serial` (firmware) installs `usb_serial_jtag`; the hub lists ports with Espressif's VID `303a` only and keys them by the USB serial (the MAC). A CH340 has no serial number and the hub does not see it at all.
- **No camera, SD, FAT or JPEG code** or dependency. The IDF has `esp_driver_sdmmc` + `fatfs`; the component registry has **`espressif/esp32-camera` 2.1.8** (2026-09-25, IDF ≥ 5.1, targets ESP32/S2/S3, depends on `esp_jpeg`), which builds on IDF v6.1 for the esp32 (§9).
- **Streams are generic in the firmware** (`fr::stream`, `ota::HttpPost`) and in the hub's route (`POST /v1/stream/in/<id>`, `StreamSink` per kind), but **CSI-only in the types**: `STREAM_KINDS = ['csi']`, `StreamInfo {kind: 'csi', format: 'csi1'}`, the live fan-out in `csi/live.ts`, the SDK's `CsiBlock` handler. The hub's `ChunkedParser` takes chunks up to 128 KB.
- `fr.display`'s `snapshot`/`snapshot_read` reads a binary blob in 1 KB BULK replies: too slow for photos (a 40 KB frame = 40 round trips); the stream route is the way.

## 1. Scope

In:
1. **The classic ESP32 as a target** (`esp32`), with a **UART0 serial backend** for `fr_serial` and **USB-UART bridge ports** on the hub (CH340 now; CP210x and FTDI by table).
2. **Board `aithinker-esp32-cam`** and profile **`e32a-cam`**; device alias **`cam-esp32`**.
3. **`fr.camera` 1.0** (`0x000B`) with driver **`dvp_camera`** (esp32-camera over the board's DVP pins) and the micro-SD as the module's own store: JPEG frames to the hub (a snapshot, a live stream, a burst), to the card (a recording that runs without the hub), or both; recordings listed, fetched and deleted from the card.
4. **Streams generalised**: a second stream kind `jpeg` (format `mjpg1`, one frame per block) through the same route, ticket, live channel and SDK; a `jpeg` sink writing files.
5. **Hub ops, CLI `fr camera …`**, and the app **`web/cam`**: live view, controls, snapshot, record-to-card, the card's recordings.
6. **T-CAMERA** and bench runs.

Out (next steps): motion detection and on-board vision (the ESP32 has no NN accelerator; a differencing detector is a later `fr.camera` feature), RTSP/HTTP MJPEG served by the board itself (the hub serves apps), audio with video, other sensors (OV5640, S3 boards: the driver is the same component, so a second board is a manifest), a dimmable flash (fr.led is on/off today).

## 2. The classic ESP32 target, serial over UART, bridge ports (D1–D4)

**D1. Target `esp32`** everywhere chips are listed, as CSI-1 D1 did for the C5: `ChipId.esp32 = 0` (`ESP_CHIP_ID_ESP32`), the hub's `CHIP_IDS` and selector `CHIPS`, `idf-version.lock` targets (`install.sh` then installs the xtensa toolchain, already present), codegen's `ADC1_FULL_SCALE_MV.esp32` and `ADC_CONVERSIONS_HZ.esp32` (read from IDF headers by stream C), `targets:` in the drivers whose peripheral the ESP32 has (`led_gpio`, `led_ws2812`, `button_gpio`, `adc_oneshot`, `i2s_mic`, `i2s_amp`; **not** `adc_audio`, `wifi_csi` *inferred* unsupported on the ESP32's MAC v1 CSI (`SOC_WIFI_CSI_SUPPORT` is set, but the record format differs; left out until tried), `espnow_tx` yes). The sim's board lists are unchanged. Download-mode restart: the ESP32 has **no `FORCE_DOWNLOAD_BOOT` bit** (`rtc_cntl_reg.h` read): `can_enter_bootloader()` is false on it, and the bridge's DTR/RTS (D4) does that job.

**D2. `fr_serial` over UART0** when the board says `console: uart0`: codegen emits `CONFIG_FR_SERIAL_UART=y` (`CONFIG_ESP_CONSOLE_UART_DEFAULT` stays the IDF default), and `fr_serial` builds its backend on `driver/uart` (UART0, 115200 8N1, RX ring 2 KB, TX ring 8 KB, `uart_vfs_dev_use_driver(0)` so stray prints queue behind the writer as with USJ), the same writer and reader tasks, the same line protocol (`@fr1:` frames, logs). **115200 proposed** for v1: the ROM and the bootloader print at 115200 and the hub opens the port once; the camera's bytes never go over serial. (CH340 and the hub could do 921600 later, with the boot lines unreadable at that rate.)

**D3. Bridge ports on the hub.** `serial.ts` lists Espressif ports as today **plus a table of USB-UART bridges** `BRIDGES = {1a86:7523 ch340, 1a86:55d4 ch9102, 10c4:ea60 cp210x, 0403:6001 ft232}` (`packages/protocol` so the CLI shows the kind). A bridge port has no MAC in its USB serial: its key is **`path:<basename>`** (`path:usbserial-110`), `usb_serial` null, `ports_seen` keyed the same; its **device id is learned from the serial link**: the first `@fr1:` envelope's source (the HELLO's device id) names it, kept until the port closes (`fr ports ls` shows `device_id` once known, `bridge: ch340`). Everything that takes a port selector (`fr flash`, `fr diag`, `fr devices provision --port`, leases) accepts the path or `path:…`. Provisioning a bridge port: the esptool probe (`--before default-reset`, D4) reads the MAC, the rest is unchanged (the registry row is by MAC as ever).

**D4. Reset and download mode on a bridge.** The hub opens every port with DTR and RTS deasserted (the board runs). On a bridge, `serial.hardReset` pulses **RTS (EN) low for 100 ms with DTR high** (the CAM-MB's transistor pair: the classic esptool "run" sequence), and `devices reset --mode bootloader` runs esptool's download sequence (EN low, IO0 low, EN high, IO0 high) through the same two lines; flashing and probing pass `before: default-reset` on a bridge (`no-reset` stays for a USJ port the hub put into download mode itself). A board on a bare FTDI (no auto-reset wiring) still needs a hand on IO0: the error says so when the probe finds no chip.

## 4. `fr.camera` (D7–D10)

**D7. `fr.camera` 1.0** (`0x000B`, `schema/capabilities/camera.cap.yaml`):
- **types:** `FrameSize {qvga 0 (320×240), vga 1 (640×480), svga 2 (800×600), xga 3 (1024×768), sxga 4 (1280×1024), uxga 5 (1600×1200)}`; `Target {hub 0, sd 1, both 2}`; `StreamEndReason {stopped, done, net_lost, overflow, error}` (overflow: the card is full or failed mid-stream); `SdState {none 0, mounted 1, error 2}`.
- **traits:** `sensor` u8 (1 ov2640), `max_frame_size` FrameSize (uxga).
- **config:** `frame_size` (svga), `max_frame_size` (uxga: the frame buffers are sized for it at init, §9), `quality` u8 12 [4, 63] (JPEG, lower is better), `fb_count` u8 2 [1, 3], `vflip` bool false, `hmirror` bool false, `brightness`/`contrast`/`saturation` i8 0 [-2, 2], `xclk_hz` u32 20,000,000 [8M, 24M], `sd` bool true (mount the card at boot; a missing card is `SdState.none`, not an error).
- **properties:** `frames` u32 (captured since boot), `fps_x10` u16 (over the last second, 0 idle), `last_bytes` u32, `streaming` bool, `sd` SdState, `sd_free_mb` u32 (0 when none).
- **events:** `stream_ended {stream_id u32, reason, frames u32, dropped u32, bytes u32}`.
- **commands** (all reliable, dispatch never blocks, as fr.csi's): `stream_start {stream_id u32, seconds u16 (0 until stop), interval_ms u32 (0: as fast as the sensor and the path allow), max_frames u32 (0: no limit), to Target = hub, frame_size? FrameSize, quality? u8}` (BUSY while one runs; INVALID_ARGS for `to sd|both` with no card); `stream_stop {stream_id}` (idempotent; NOT_FOUND for another id); `set {frame_size?, quality?, vflip?, hmirror?, brightness?, contrast?, saturation?}` (idempotent; applied to the sensor now and kept until reboot; INVALID_ARGS past `max_frame_size`); `sd_list {from u32 = 0}` → result `{recordings array<{id u32, frames u32, bytes u32}> ≤ 64, next u32 (0: none)}` (BULK, oldest first); `sd_fetch {stream_id u32, recording u32, from_frame u32 = 0}` (streams a recording's frames to the hub as an mjpg1 stream, `flags` b0 set; BUSY while a stream runs; NOT_FOUND); `sd_delete {recording}` (idempotent); `sd_format` is **not** offered (`visibility: test` at most; the Mac formats cards).

**D8. The capture loop** (`CameraService`, L2, over `hal::Camera` + `hal::BlockStore` *names C fixes*): a stream is a `fr::stream` worker pass like CSI's: each pass grabs one frame (`esp_camera_fb_get` with `CAMERA_GRAB_LATEST`, so a slow path takes the newest frame, never a backlog), then by `to`: **hub** → one mjpg1 block through `HttpPost` in `kSendSliceMs` slices (a block not gone in `kStallMs` 3 s ends it `net_lost`); **sd** → `/sdcard/fr/<stream_id 8 hex>/<index 6 digits>.jpg` written whole (`fwrite` + `fsync` every 8 frames **proposed**), the directory made at start; **both** → the card first, then the hub; a hub send that fails while writing to the card ends the stream `net_lost` with what the card holds listed as a recording. `interval_ms` paces by the frame's start time (a 1,000 ms timelapse takes a frame every second, not every second plus the capture time); `seconds` and `max_frames` end it `done`. `dropped` counts frames the sensor delivered that the stream could not take (the hub path behind). Frames never cross the hub link (serial or TCP); only the stream route carries them.

**D9. The driver `dvp_camera`** (`hardware/drivers/dvp_camera`): `implements: hal.Camera`, `class fr::drv::DvpCamera`, `targets [esp32, esp32s3]` (the S3 cam clone can follow with a manifest), `binds: cameras`, `accepts_kinds [dvp]`, config from `board.*` (the 16 pins and `xclk_hz`), `dependencies {espressif/esp32-camera: "==2.1.8"}` (+ its `esp_jpeg`), `sdkconfig [CONFIG_CAMERA_CORE0=y]` **proposed** (the camera task on core 0 with Wi-Fi; `fr_net` is pinned to core 0 on the S3; C decides from the S3's pattern), `resources {gpio: 16, i2s: 1 (the ESP32's camera DMA is I2S0), ledc_timers: 1, ledc_channels: 1 (XCLK)}`. The card: driver **`sdmmc_card`** (`implements: hal.BlockStore`, `binds: sd_cards`, `resources {gpio: 3 (1-bit), sdmmc_slot: 1}`, `targets [esp32, esp32s3]`): mounts FAT (`esp_vfs_fat_sdmmc_mount`, 1-bit, internal pull-ups, 20 MHz, no auto-format), reports state and free space, lists/removes recordings; a card inserted after boot is not detected (**proposed**: `sd_mount` later if wanted).

**D10. The wire, format `mjpg1`** (`X-Fr-Kind: jpeg`, `X-Fr-Format: mjpg1`): each HTTP chunk is **one frame**, head 24 B, integers LE:

| offset | bytes | field |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 4 | `seq` u32: chunks of this stream from 0 |
| 4 | 4 | `index` u32: the frame's index in the stream, dropped ones counted |
| 8 | 4 | `t_us` u32: the chip's µs clock at capture |
| 12 | 2 | `width` u16 |
| 14 | 2 | `height` u16 |
| 16 | 1 | `quality` u8 |
| 17 | 1 | `frame_size` u8 (FrameSize) |
| 18 | 2 | `flags` u16: b0 from the card (sd_fetch), others 0 |
| 20 | 4 | `len` u32: JPEG bytes (SOI … EOI) |
| 24 | len | the JPEG |

A frame is at most **`kFrameMaxBytes` 512 KB** (UXGA at quality 4 *inferred* under 300 KB); the hub's chunk parser limit rises to 1 MB for this kind (`MAX_CHUNK_BYTES` per kind). The hub's **jpeg sink** writes each frame as `<dir>/<index 6 digits>.jpg` (or a single `out` file for a one-frame stream), counts frames, dropped (index jumps), bytes, and hands the block to the live fan-out. A `.mjpg` container is not written (a directory of JPEGs is what every tool reads; `ffmpeg -pattern_type glob` makes a video).

(absolute .jpg under FR_HOME; default `captures/<alias>/<yyyymmdd-hhmmss>.jpg`), frame_size?, quality?, flash? bool, wait_s?}` (a job of one frame: `max_frames 1`; `flash` sets the board's `flash` fr.led on before and off after, when the profile has one) → `{device, alias, path, bytes, width, height, ms}`; `camera_stream {device, instance?, seconds 0..3600, interval_ms, max_frames, to (hub|sd|both), out? **D14. Files on the Mac** under `FR_HOME/captures/<alias>/…` by default (`out` overrides; absolute under FR_HOME as `.csi`'s); `fr doctor` lists the directory; nothing is deleted by the hub.

## 6. Gates (D15; numbers proposed)

**T-CAMERA** (`tests/e2e/t-camera.ts`):
1. **Snap:** `camera_snap` at svga: a file with SOI/EOI, the JPEG's SOF0 says 800×600, ≤ 2 s end to end; ten in a row, every one valid.
2. **Live:** 20 s at svga, `interval_ms 0`, to the hub: ≥ 5 frames/s (the classic ESP32's Wi-Fi: a 43 KB frame in under 200 ms), dropped ≤ 2 %, every frame valid, index contiguous but for the counted drops.
3. **Record and fetch:** 10 frames at 1 s to the card (`to sd`), the hub away for it (the stream ends `done` with no POST), then `sd_list` names it with 10 frames, `sd_fetch` brings 10 identical files (bytes equal), `sd_delete` removes it.
4. **Settings:** `set` xga then a snap says 1024×768; `set` past `max_frame_size` is INVALID_ARGS.
5. **Bridge port:** `fr ports ls` shows the CH340 port with the device id learned from HELLO; `fr devices reset cam-esp32` reboots it (boot banner seen); `fr flash`'s probe works through the bridge.
6. **Host:** the mjpg1 parser and sink (files, drops), `parseJpegBlock`, the bridge table and port keys, the UART backend in the host sim where the serial path is built, codegen for the esp32 target and the new sections.
- Regressions: T-CODEC (a new capability), the CSI stream tests (the generalised types), T-PROV on a bridge.

## 7. Risks

- **Internal RAM on the classic ESP32** (D6): the camera's DMA buffers (32 KB), Wi-Fi's buffers and the framework's tables all want the ~300 KB of DRAM; the frame buffers are in PSRAM. Mitigation: the heap figure at first boot decides `memory: small`.
- **Wi-Fi throughput:** the ESP32's TCP tops out around 1 MB/s in theory, a few hundred KB/s with the hub's HTTP in slices: SVGA at 5–10 fps, XGA at 2–4. The gate asks for 5 at svga; the live view is a monitor, not video.
- **The SD card and GPIO12/2/4:** 1-bit mode avoids 12 and 13; GPIO2 (D0) is a strapping pin the card pulls up (download mode needs it low: the CAM-MB's IO0-only wiring does not touch it, and the ROM accepts 2 high *inferred*); GPIO4 shared by the flash LED and the card's unused D1 (left as an output, low).
- **UART0 at 115200** for logs and the serial link: the Wi-Fi log at boot is a few KB; fine. OTA goes over Wi-Fi.
- **Opening the CH340 port resets the board** on some systems (DTR toggles): the hub deasserts DTR/RTS on open; if a boot shows up at every open, the base's circuit is the cause and the note says so.
- **The flash LED** draws ~100 mA pulses; on the base's USB supply it browned the board out in other people's reports *inferred*; the gate keeps it short.

## 9. Spike (2026-11-03, `camspike` in the scratchpad, flashed over the base's CH340 at 460800)

IDF v6.1, target esp32, `espressif/esp32-camera` 2.1.8 (+ `esp_jpeg` 1.3.x), `esp_driver_sdmmc` + `fatfs`, PSRAM quad 40 MHz: builds (388 KB app, no deprecation warnings in the component). On the board:
- PSRAM: 4 MB found and tested (`SPI SRAM memory test OK`), 32 KB of internal RAM reserved for DMA; 350 KB internal free before the camera, 311 KB after it.
- Camera: `cam init ok` in 228 ms, OV2640 at SCCB address 0x30 (PID 0x26, VER 0x42, MIDH 0x7F, MIDL 0xA2); two frame buffers of 96,000 B in PSRAM for svga.
- JPEG frames (quality 12): qvga 9–13 KB in 19 ms; vga 21–34 KB in 39 ms; svga 31–54 KB in 38 ms; xga 71–80 KB in 79 ms; **uxga 83 KB in 80 ms once, then `FB-OVF` for every frame** (the buffers were sized for svga at init: `max_frame_size`, D7). A steady svga loop runs at **25.2 fps**, 42–44 KB a frame, the internal heap steady at 311 KB.
- SD: the 32 GB SDHC mounts in 63 ms, 1-bit, 20 MHz; a 20,516 B frame written and read back the same size in 107 ms; unmount clean.
- A `NO-EOI` warning once at the first vga frame (the size change): the driver's own retry gave a whole frame.

- **The store (ServiceFacts.store).** A service may declare a second, optional hardware dependency: `SERVICES['fr.camera'].store = { implements: 'hal.BlockStore', binds: 'sd_cards' }`. The profile names it beside `bind`: `cam: { cap: fr.camera, driver: dvp_camera, bind: cameras.main, store: { driver: sdmmc_card, bind: sd_cards.card } }`. `resolveStore` checks the driver implements the store contract, supports the target, `binds` the section the service names and accepts the entry's kind; its Config comes from the entry (`from: board.<key>`) and driver.yaml defaults only (no profile overrides, no `bind`/`bind.<key>`, `i2s_port`, `instance` or `gpio_mask` fields: refused in the driver.yaml); its gpio fields are the instance's pins (reserved-group rule under its own bind, strapping warnings, double claims), its `resources` join the instance's budget (the card's `sdmmc_slot`), its `sdkconfig` lines and component join the profile's. The glue: `kStoreConfig`, `fr::drv::SdmmcCard store{kStoreConfig};`, `fr::CameraService service{driver, &store, kConfig};`; without a store on a service that takes one, `service{driver, nullptr, kConfig}`. The manifest cap carries `store: { driver, bind }`; `FirmwareResult.instances[name].store` is `{ driver, bind, pins, driverConfig }`. A `store` on a capability without one is refused ("fr.led takes no store (capabilities with one: fr.camera)").

**HAL** (fr_core, IDF-free):
```cpp
// fr/hal/camera.hpp
enum class FrameSize : uint8_t { qvga = 0, vga, svga, xga, sxga, uxga };     // fr.camera FrameSize, same values
struct FrameDims { uint16_t width, height; }; constexpr FrameDims frame_dims(FrameSize);
inline constexpr size_t kFrameMaxBytes = 512 * 1024;
inline constexpr uint8_t kSensorUnknown = 0, kSensorOv2640 = 1;
struct CameraSettings { FrameSize frame_size = svga; uint8_t quality = 12; bool vflip, hmirror; int8_t brightness, contrast, saturation; };
struct CameraInit { FrameSize max_frame_size = uxga; uint8_t fb_count = 2; uint32_t xclk_hz = 20'000'000; CameraSettings settings; };
struct Frame { std::span<const uint8_t> jpeg; uint16_t width, height; uint32_t t_us; void *handle; };  // valid until release()
class Camera {
    Status init(Core &);                              // board init; touches no hardware
    Status start(const CameraInit &);                 // core task: power, XCLK, SCCB probe, buffers for max_frame_size; Err::failed, Err::busy
    Status stop(); bool running() const;
    Status grab(Frame &, uint32_t timeout_ms);        // fr_stream task; newest frame (CAMERA_GRAB_LATEST); Err::busy none in time, Err::failed sensor
    void release(Frame &);                            // fr_stream task; esp_camera_fb_return
    Status set(const CameraSettings &);               // core or fr_stream task, never beside a grab; Err::invalid_args past max_frame_size
    uint8_t sensor() const; uint32_t frames() const; uint32_t failed() const; Selftest selftest();
};
// fr/hal/block_store.hpp
enum class StoreState : uint8_t { none = 0, mounted = 1, error = 2 };      // fr.camera CardState, same values
struct Recording { uint32_t id, frames, bytes; };
class BlockStore {
    Status init(Core &);                              // board init: the mount; no card is state none, OK
    StoreState state() const; bool mounted() const; uint32_t free_mb() const;
    Status open_recording(uint32_t id);               // fr_stream task; Err::busy one open, Err::unsupported not mounted, Err::failed full
    Status write_frame(uint32_t index, std::span<const uint8_t> jpeg);
    Status close_recording();
    Status list(uint32_t from, std::span<Recording> out, uint32_t &count, uint32_t &next);   // core task, a few ms
    Status info(uint32_t id, Recording &out);                                                 // Err::not_found
    Status read_frame(uint32_t id, uint32_t index, std::span<uint8_t> buf, uint32_t &len);    // fr_stream task
    Status remove(uint32_t id);                                                               // core task; OK when gone, Err::busy while open
    Selftest selftest();
};
```
Beyond D9's list the store has `close_recording()` (the last fsync) and `info(id)` (sd_fetch's bounds), and `Camera` separates `start()` from `init()` (init touches no hardware, spec 03 §3.4; the service calls start at its own init). Threading is in each header: start/stop/set and list/info/remove on the core task, grab/release and the recording I/O on the fr_stream task, `set` never beside a `grab` (the service applies a `set` that arrives during a stream from its next pass).

## 11. Bench round (2026-11-03)

- **The base and the cable:** two micro-USB cables carried power only; the third enumerated the CAM-MB's CH340 (`1a86:7523`, `/dev/cu.usbserial-110`). esptool read an ESP32-D0WD-V3 rev 3.1, 40 MHz, 4 MB flash; the ROM has no chip id ("Reading MAC address instead"); MAC `80:f3:da:61:44:d4`.
- **Download mode and the SD card (found the hard way):** after the spike had used the card, every reset with IO0 low booted with `boot:0xb (HSPI_FLASH_BOOT)` and `invalid header`, and esptool said "Wrong boot mode detected (0xb)". Boot mode 0xb is IO0 low **with GPIO2 high**: the card drives DAT0 (GPIO2) high once it has been initialised, unmount or not, until a power cycle. The ROM then tries an HSPI flash instead of entering download mode. Serial flashing of an ESP32-CAM whose card has been used needs a power cycle with the card out (the sidecar's hint now says so); OTA over Wi-Fi is unaffected (a normal reboot boots `0x1b` whatever GPIO2 does, as the spike's own resets showed). A firmware step that puts the card back to idle (CMD0) before a serial flash is the follow-up (§12).
- **The sidecar's probe** asked the ROM for its security info (command 0x14), which the ESP32 ROM does not have: the probe now reports `security_info: unsupported` and goes on.
- **Provisioning:** `fr devices provision --port /dev/cu.usbserial-110 --profile e32a-cam --alias cam-esp32` (card out): 106 s, reset through the hub's RTS pulse, HELLO over serial (the UART0 backend and the bridge's device-id learning work: `fr ports ls` shows `80f3da6144d4 /dev/cu.usbserial-110 ch340`), Wi-Fi joined, TCP. Device `cam-esp32`, addr 10. The image: 1,489,040 B (75.7 % of an OTA slot).
- **First checks (the review's fixes not yet flashed):** `fr camera status`: sensor ov2640, max uxga, sd none (card out). `fr camera snap`: 800×600, 27,103 B, **157 ms** end to end. `fr camera stream --seconds 15`: **186 frames, 12.3 fps, 0 dropped**, 5.1 MB (340 KB/s), every file a whole JPEG of 800×600. `web/cam`: live view at 9–11 fps, 0 drops, 0 hub gaps; the flash LED from the app lights the room (frames grow to 40 KB). The serial link: 0 bad frames, 0 drops at 115200 beside the stream.
- **Heap:** `diag info` with Wi-Fi up and the camera started: **38.8 KB internal free**, PSRAM 3,330 KB free: at D6's threshold. To try: `memory: small` (−16 KB of .bss on the other profiles) against the stream rate.
- **Seen, to fix:** a `set frame_size` while a live view runs does not change the live frames, because `web/cam` starts its view with the setting as the stream's own override (the stream's size wins for its length, as §10 says): the app should restart the view on a size or quality change. `camera_stream`'s result carries a `snap` object for a plain stream (the first frame's facts under a misleading name). `web/cam` calls `sd_list` with no card and shows the UNSUPPORTED error under "no card".
- **The card (after a reboot with it back in):** `sd mounted`; `camera_stream --to sd --interval-ms 1000 --seconds 10`, the hub away from the frames: `device_frames 10`, 307,777 B, `recording 941721632`; `sd ls` lists it; `sd_fetch` brought **10 files, 307,777 B, byte for byte the recording** (1.7 s, 5.9 fps over the stream); `sd_delete` left 0 recordings. `fr camera status` after the reboot by port path: `sd mounted (2963 MB free)` on a 32 GB card: the free figure is wrong (overflow or the wrong unit; to fix).
- **Seen, to fix (host):** `fr camera sd fetch|rm <device> <recording>` passes the id as a string (INVALID_ARGS from the op: the generated CLI does not coerce a numeric positional); `fr devices reset cam-esp32` answers NOT_FOUND although `fr ports ls` shows the bridge port with the learned id (by `path:usbserial-110` it works: the alias → bridge-port lookup is missing); `camera_sd_fetch`'s and `camera_stream`'s results carry a `snap` object.
- **The review's fixes on the board (build `aeeb9dad749a`, 1,491,104 B, 75.8 %):** the first `fr ota cam-esp32` picked the serial route (the board sits on a bridge port) and failed with boot mode 0xb as above; `--via wifi` updated it in 27 s (download 5.7 s). Snap 800×600, 39,463 B, 181 ms. `camera_stream --seconds 15`: 136 frames, 9.1 fps, 0 dropped, 5.17 MB: the same **~345 KB/s** as the first round with frames of 38 KB in evening light instead of 27 KB, so the Wi-Fi path, not the firmware, sets the rate. Card: `--to sd --max-frames 10 --interval-ms 200` → `device_frames 10, device_dropped 1`, 9 files of 365,176 B: with an interval, a slot that goes by while the card still writes the previous frame counts as dropped and `max_frames` counts slots (D8; the first write makes two directories and `meta.bin`, over 200 ms at 1-bit). `fr camera sd fetch cam-esp32 941721638` and `sd rm` by the CLI (the numeric positional now coerced): 9 whole 800×600 JPEGs on the Mac; the delete answers at once and the listing shows 0 recordings on the next `sd ls` (fr_stream removes it). `fr devices reset cam-esp32` by alias: a hard reset over the bridge, the board back with the card mounted (after a hub restart: the hub had been running the pre-fix code). `web/cam`: a frame-size change restarts the live view (svga 9 fps → vga 13 fps, "set frame_size vga; restarting the live view").

## 12. Follow-ups

- **Release the card before a serial flash:** a diag or flashing step that sends CMD0 (go idle) to the card so DAT0 (GPIO2) floats low again, letting `fr flash`/provision over the bridge work with the card in; until then the pytools hint says to power-cycle with the card out.
- **A `tasks` diag command** (`uxTaskGetSystemState`, needs `CONFIG_FREERTOS_USE_TRACE_FACILITY`): the stack high-water marks of fr_stream, fr_core and the audio engine, to size `kStackBytes` from a measurement instead of a guess.
- **A memory class between `default` and `small`** (codegen `memory:`): the FR_* table shrinks only, no IRAM moves and no Wi-Fi buffer caps, for a board with PSRAM and a stream (this one: 36 KB free at idle).
- **The free-space figure:** check the 32 GB card on the Mac; if it is near empty, set `FF_FS_NOFSINFO` to recount free clusters at mount (slow on a large FAT32: measure).
- **`sd_mount` for a hot insert** (the card is probed at init only; today a card put in later needs a reboot).
- **A dimmable flash** (LEDC on GPIO4) and a motion-detection mode (frame difference on a qvga grab, an event with a snap).
- **An S3 camera board manifest** (`esp32s3-wroom1-cam-n16r8` has the DVP pins) for a second `fr.camera` device and the `both`-target soak.
- **Serial at 921600** on the bridge (the CH340 takes it) for faster provisioning and the serial OTA route.
- **`portSel`'s description** in the op schema should name `path:<basename>` beside `usb:` and the device id.
