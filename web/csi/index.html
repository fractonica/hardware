One voice per band of subcarriers, low bands as low notes. The room's subcarrier profile sets the chord; a band swells when its subcarriers change; motion brightens it and adds a shimmer. A still room holds a quiet chord. Raw pitch drops the scale: each voice bends up or down as its band's signal rises or falls. Parrot listens for a while, then speaks what it heard as one compressed word; a still room gets silence. Laughter waits for movement, records it until it stops, then laughs at it: the ha-ha rate, pitch and vowel follow how you moved. Babble does the same in an invented language: longer syllables, four consonants and five vowels chosen by the movement, with pauses between words where it dipped.
